Welcome to the official support page for Batch Image Studio. We are here to help you get the most out of your image editing and photo printing workflow.

Product Features

Batch Edit

Import multiple images and edit them in one workflow. Resize, rotate, flip, adjust brightness, contrast and saturation, convert images to black and white, and export processed images.

Photo Layout

Arrange multiple photos on document pages. Customize paper size, orientation, image placement, margins, spacing and captions. Preview and export multi-page PDF documents.

Print Studio

Arrange multiple images for printing with configurable paper size, orientation, image fit, margins and spacing. Preview your layout and print using the macOS print system.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my original images stored?

Batch Image Studio works with your selected local images. Original files are kept unchanged during non-destructive editing.

How do I export edited images?

In Batch Edit, select the images you want to process, configure your export settings and choose an output folder.

How do I create a photo PDF?

Open Photo Layout, import your images, configure the document and page layout, preview your pages and select Export PDF.

Can I print multiple photos on one page?

Yes. Open Print Studio and configure the paper size, orientation and number of photos per page.

What should I do if an export fails?

Check that the destination folder is accessible and that sufficient disk space is available. If the problem continues, contact our support team with details of the issue.
