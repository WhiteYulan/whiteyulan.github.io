Privacy Policy

Batch Image Studio for macOS

Introduction

Batch Image Studio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use our macOS application.

Our goal is to provide image editing, photo layout and printing tools while keeping your personal files under your control.

Information We Collect

Batch Image Studio does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its core features.

The application is designed to process images locally on your Mac. We do not collect or upload your original images, processed images, or document contents to our servers.

Local Files and Image Processing

You choose which images and folders the application can access. Image editing, resizing, rotation, adjustments, photo layout and PDF export are performed locally on your Mac.

Your original image files are not intentionally modified or uploaded by the application. Projects and exported files are stored in locations you choose on your device.

Data Storage and Security

Application workspace data and project information are stored locally on your Mac.

Batch Image Studio does not operate a cloud account system for storing your images or projects. Please keep your own backups of important files.

Third-Party Services

Batch Image Studio does not include third-party advertising services or require third-party analytics accounts.

macOS system services, such as file access and printing, may be used when you choose to use those features. Their operation is subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies and system settings.

Children's Privacy

Batch Image Studio is a general-purpose productivity application and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its features change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
